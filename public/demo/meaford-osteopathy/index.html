Meaford Osteopathy

A small clinic providing osteopathic treatment in downtown Meaford.



Osteopathic Manual Therapists can treat a wide spectrum of conditions. Treatment is founded on the idea that a mechanically sound body is a well functioning body. When bones, soft tissues and muscles are balanced, the blood vessels and nerves that weave through them can operate well. A body in balance heals more effectively and results in greater well-being.

Osteopathic Manual Therapy can help with:

  • Back, Neck & Joint Pain
  • Pregnancy & Postpartum
  • Pre & Post Surgical Care
  • Stress & Overall Wellbeing
  • Injury Prevention & Recovery
  • ...and more
